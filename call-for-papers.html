Call for Papers

We invite submissions to the Universality and Dynamics in High-Dimensional Learning and Inference Workshop at ISIT 2026. This workshop brings together researchers in information theory, machine learning, high-dimensional statistics, random matrix theory, and statistical physics to develop a unified view of recent advances in high-dimensional learning and inference.

Join us in Guangzhou for a full-day workshop on Universality and Dynamics in High-Dimensional Learning and Inference at ISIT 2026.

Important Dates

  • Paper submission deadline April 7, 2026
  • Acceptance notification April 21, 2026
  • Final manuscript deadline April 28, 2026
  • Workshop date July 3, 2026
