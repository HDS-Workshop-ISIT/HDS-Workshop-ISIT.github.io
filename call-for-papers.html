Call for Papers

We invite submissions to the Universality and Dynamics in High-Dimensional Learning and Inference Workshop at ISIT 2026. This workshop brings together researchers in information theory, machine learning, high-dimensional statistics, random matrix theory, and statistical physics to develop a unified view of recent advances in high-dimensional learning and inference.

Two Submission Routes
Route 1

Paper Submission (Proceedings)

Submit a workshop paper through EDAS if you would like your work to be reviewed for inclusion in the official workshop proceedings.

Review required · Proceedings eligible · Final manuscript required if accepted

Submit via EDAS
Route 2

Poster-Only Submission (Non-Proceedings)

Submit through the Google Form if you would like to present a poster without a formal paper. This route is not included in the proceedings.

Poster session only · No proceedings paper · Workshop registration required

Submit via Google Form

Authors may choose either of the two routes above: a paper submission route for proceedings consideration, or a poster-only route for workshop presentation without a formal paper.

Route 2: Poster-Only Submission (Non-Proceedings)

We also welcome poster-only submissions without a formal paper. This route is intended for authors who would like to present their work during the workshop poster session but do not wish to submit a proceedings paper.

  • Proceedings status Poster-only submissions will not be included in the proceedings.
  • Workshop registration Poster presenters must register for the workshop.
  • Submission process Please complete the Google Form with the requested information, including author information, poster title, and poster file.
  • Deadline There is currently no strict deadline for poster-only submissions, though early submission is encouraged for planning purposes.
  • Poster format Detailed poster size and formatting requirements will be posted on this page later.
